LIMONEEZ is a sneaker brand in the making, driven by a simple belief: everyday footwear can have its own point of view. We're exploring clean shapes, confident colour and details that make a pair feel unmistakably yours.

These first designs are prototypes — the beginning of a bigger idea. Follow the journey as we shape what's next.
